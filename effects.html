Effects

What are the positive and negative effects of generative AI?

Negative Effects

Environmental impacts

The main reason why generative AI is negative is its environmental pollution. As demand for generative AI rises, expanding facilities are drawing heavily on water supplies. The United States has approximately 4,767 to 5,098 total data center facilities, many are placed in states with frequent drought. Contaminated tap water, water bill spikes, and heat stress are all impacts of data center infrastructure. Training generative AI also takes significant water usage. Training GPT-3 in Microsoft's U.S. data centers, is estimated to consume a total of 5.4 million liters. This is only expected to get worse as generative AI intensifies. Additionally, generative AI has caused massive amounts of e-waste. This can include discarded GPUs, CPUs, and batteries. If disposed improperly, hazardous substances like lead, mercury, and arsenic can disrupt local ecosystems. Overall, the harmful environmental impacts caused by generative AI raises concerns for how its pollution will effect the future.

Cognitive impacts

Generative AI negatively affects the environment, but it also negatively affects the users of it. AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Open AI, and Google Gemini have the ability to write essays, emails, and more. Because of this, it can be easy to rely on LLMs (Large Language Models) to do the work for us, as it requires little cognitive effort. Studies have already shown that young people might be particularly vulnerable to the negative effects that using AI can have on key cognitive skills like critical thinking. This can have dangerous implications for the future, as it can limit people's abilities to learn, process information, and even perform in occupations.

Positive effects

After looking at the negative effects of generative AI, it does provide many positive effects. Apart from the basic boosting of productivity when coding, analyzing data, and summarising information, generative AI can help people. Moxi, a hospital robot, uses generative AI to quickly learn what to do in a new hospital. Because of this, Moxi helps hospitals run 24/7 by assisting staff with tasks such as running patient supplies, delivering lab samples, fetching items from central supply, distributing PPE, and delivering medications. This shows that generative AI can co-exist with humans in an ethical way, and be used to help people. Overall, generative AI can be used to increase productivity, but it shouldn't eliminate any form of critically thinking and creativity in the process. The main takeaway is that generative AI can be used positively, but we often misuse its purpose.
